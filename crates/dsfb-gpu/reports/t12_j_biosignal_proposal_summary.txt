================================================================
T.12.j - Medical / Biosignal amendment proposal summary
================================================================

Panel-locked thesis. T.12.j files the Medical / Biosignal
amendment proposal. It admits only deterministic biosignal
witnesses whose signal source, sampling law, filtering law,
morphology measurement law, baseline / noise handling, artifact
confuser profile, and decision functional are declared;
resolves SEED collisions; classifies measurement variants as
parameterizations or domain transfers; rejects diagnostic
classifiers and learned arrhythmia scores unless
deterministically reduced; and preserves the frozen T.10
corpus hash.

Main panel warning: "Count signal witnesses, not diagnoses.
No sampling / filtering / morphology law, no canonical
admission."

Panel-locked non-claim (MUST appear verbatim):

> T.12.j does not admit medical diagnoses. It admits
> deterministic biosignal witnesses: morphology, interval,
> artifact, and spectral signal structures under declared
> sampling, filtering, and measurement laws. Clinical
> interpretation remains out of scope.

measurement" -- NEVER as a clinical diagnosis. Pinned by
t12_j_rejects_diagnostic_claim_language which scans every such
reason for forbidden diagnostic terms (arrhythmia, fibrillation,
infarction, ischemia, ischaemia, tachycardia, bradycardia,

RejectedNotDeterministic reason text (where they describe what
is NOT admitted) or in rejection-shell display names.
