----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel-required load-bearing negatives (8)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R.1  DetectorSpecForUnratifiedProposal
     Reject any decision whose claimed source authority is
     T12RatifiedAndPassported AND whose canonical id is NOT in
     the ratified expansion index AND whose eligibility is NOT
     RejectedUnratifiedProposal. Surfaces the "ratified claim
     for an id that was never ratified" defect at the registry-
     generation boundary.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_for_unratified_proposal

R.2  DetectorSpecForMissingFf1Passport
     Reject any decision whose claimed source authority is
     T12RatifiedAndPassported AND whose canonical id IS in
     the ratified expansion index AND NOT in the FF.1 passport
     index AND whose eligibility is NOT
     RejectedMissingFf1Passport. Mirror of FF.2's
     MissingPassport rule at the registry-generation boundary.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_for_missing_ff1_passport

R.3  DetectorSpecWhenCorpusHashV2Mismatch
     Reject when the gate's pinned corpus_hash_v2 does not
     equal the live consolidation report's corpus_hash_v2.
     The ratification anchor itself drifted; every ratified
     claim is invalidated.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_when_corpus_hash_v2_mismatch

R.4  DetectorSpecWhenPassportIndexHashMismatch
     Reject when the gate's pinned ff1_passport_index_hash_v1
     does not equal the live FF.1 passport-index hash. The
     passport authority drifted.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_when_passport_index_hash_mismatch

R.5  DetectorSpecFromAdHocRecord
     Reject any decision whose claimed source authority is
     AdHocUnsanctioned AND whose eligibility is NOT
     RejectedAdHocRecord. Surfaces records proposed for
     registry generation outside the panel-locked two-source
     discipline.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_from_ad_hoc_record

R.6  DetectorSpecWithUnknownSourceAuthority
     Reject any decision whose claimed source authority is
     UnknownExternal AND whose eligibility is NOT
     RejectedUnknownSourceAuthority. Surfaces records with
     no SEED / no ratified passport / no panel-locked path.
     Test: ff3_rejects_detector_spec_with_unknown_source_authority

R.7  RegistryGenerationThatSkipsFf2RatificationGate
     Reject when the gate's pinned
     ff2_activation_ratification_gate_hash_v1 does not equal
     the live FF.2 gate hash. Panel verdict: FF.3 MUST consult
     the FF.2 ratification gate; any FF.3 build that skips or
     stales the FF.2 anchor is a defect.
     Test: ff3_rejects_registry_generation_that_skips_ff2_ratification_gate

R.8  RegistryGenerationThatMutatesExistingRegistryHash
     Reject when the FF.3 eligible_count exceeds the FF.2
     gate's eligible count (SeedHistorical +
     T12RatifiedAndPassported). FF.3 cannot admit MORE
     candidates for registry generation than FF.2 admits for
     activation; doing so would silently mutate the would-be
     registry surface beyond the FF.2-ratified set.
     Test: ff3_rejects_registry_generation_that_mutates_existing_registry_hash

Structural defect rules (7)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R.9  DuplicateGateDecisionForSameCanonicalId
     Reject two decisions for the same canonical id.
     Test: ff3_rejects_duplicate_gate_decision_for_same_canonical_id

R.10 GateDecisionsNotSortedAscending
     Reject when decisions are not sorted strictly ascending
     by canonical id.
     Test: ff3_rejects_gate_decisions_not_sorted_ascending

R.11 CorpusHashV1Mismatch
     Reject when the gate's corpus_hash_v1 does not equal
     compute_corpus_hash_v1().
     Test: ff3_rejects_corpus_hash_v1_mismatch

R.12 ConsolidationReportHashV1Mismatch
     Reject when the gate's consolidation_report_hash_v1 does
     not equal the live consolidation report hash.
     Test: ff3_rejects_consolidation_report_hash_v1_mismatch

R.13 EligibleDecisionCarriesNonEmptyRejectionReason
     Reject when an Eligible decision carries a non-empty
     rejection-reason wire name.
     Test: ff3_rejects_eligible_decision_with_nonempty_rejection_reason

R.14 RejectionDecisionCarriesEmptyRejectionReason
     Reject when a non-Eligible decision carries an empty
     rejection-reason wire name. Every rejection MUST be
     reason-coded.
     Test: ff3_rejects_rejection_decision_with_empty_rejection_reason

R.15 DecisionClassificationInconsistentWithClaim
     Reject when a decision's claimed source authority and its
     eligibility classification are structurally inconsistent
     (e.g. a SeedHistorical claim cannot legitimately be
     classified as RejectedUnratifiedProposal — that rejection
     applies only to T12RatifiedAndPassported claims).
     Test: ff3_rejects_decision_classification_inconsistent_with_claim

R.16 SeedLengthMutated (unconditional)
     Reject when SEED.len() != 54. Covered by every default-
     build invariant test.
