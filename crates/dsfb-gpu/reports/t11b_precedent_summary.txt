    MissingCanonicalSubject     binding names a canonical_id not in SEED
    MissingAliasSubject         binding names an alias_id not in CLAIMS
    KindReasonIncompatible      AliasCollapse with role-drift reason
                                (SameFormulaDifferentWitnessRole,
                                DifferentDecisionFunctional, etc.)
                                preserves the T.3/T.4 semantic-role-
                                hash collapse invariant
    KindBindingIncompatible     Global-only kinds carry per-record bindings
                                (or vice versa)
    DuplicatePrecedentId        the collector reassigns ids after sort;
                                duplicates indicate corruption
