S1.3d Budget Pruning + Redundancy Suppression — Summary Receipt
================================================================

Panel verdict: post-FF.5 panel directive (2026-05-16). Sealed
under the 10-step per-section discipline ritual.

Commit identity (panel-locked):

    S1.3d turns the eligible, ratified, passported detector
    surface into a budget-aware deployment plan by applying
    deterministic budget pruning and redundancy suppression.

Panel-locked verdict (verbatim):

    Eligibility is not activation; activation is not budget
    admission. Budget exclusion must be explicit, reason-coded,
    and replayable.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Court-delta posture
----------------------------------------------------------------

S1.3d is the budget pruning + redundancy suppression layer
above S1.3a + FF.2 + FF.3. It consumes the FF.3-eligible
detector surface (152 ratified candidates at S1.3d baseline =
54 SEED + 98 T12-passported) plus a declared TaskBudget
envelope plus an explicit set of RedundancyCluster
declarations; emits per-candidate S13dBudgetDecision records.

Each decision carries either:

  - an Active outcome with a RetainedAsBudgetSurvivor or
    RetainedAsRepresentativeWitness retain reason, OR
  - a Disabled outcome with one of eight reason-coded budget
    disable variants:
      * DisabledByBudget
      * DisabledByRedundancy
      * DisabledByGpuFamilyQuota
      * DisabledByTaskBudget
      * DisabledByRuntimeBudget
      * DisabledByMemoryBudget
      * DisabledByContraindicationBudget
      * DisabledByCoverageHoleBudget

The reason-code separation is the load-bearing win:

  DisabledByWeakLBand           (S1.3a activation reason)
  ≠ DisabledUnratifiedProposal  (FF.2 activation reason)
  ≠ RejectedUnratifiedProposal  (FF.3 registry rejection)
  ≠ DisabledByBudget            (S1.3d budget pruning)
  ≠ DisabledByRedundancy        (S1.3d redundancy suppression)

Five different court failures the operator must be able to
distinguish.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel-locked non-claims (must appear in README + paper + plan)
----------------------------------------------------------------

S1.3d does NOT:

  - add new detectors;
  - alter any upstream hash anchor (corpus_hash_v1,
    corpus_hash_v2, every FF.1 / FF.2 / FF.3 / FF.4 / FF.5
    hash unchanged);
  - rewrite any prior T.11 / S1.3 / T.12.x / FF.x hash;
  - mutate SEED.len() (stays at 54);
  - change S1.3a SEED activation decisions, FF.2 ratification
    decisions, or FF.3 registry-generation eligibility;
  - generate CUDA kernels;
  - itself emit KernelPlan records (that is S1.3e);
  - decide contraindications or challenges;
  - modify the registry crate.

S1.3d is a deterministic decision layer above the eligibility
court; the layer's only job is reason-coded budget admission +
redundancy suppression with replayable tie-break transcripts.
