Verifier rules enforced (per precedent)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Every SingleCanonical / CanonicalToCanonical binding names
    a SEED canonical_id (rejection kind:
    MissingCanonicalSubject).
2.  Every Composition binding names a SEED canonical_id for the
    subject AND every parent.
3.  Every AliasToCanonical binding names an alias_id that exists
    in CLAIMS (rejection kind: MissingAliasSubject) AND a
    canonical_id that exists in SEED.
4.  AliasCollapse precedents MUST carry a "same role" alias
    reason. Reasons that imply semantic-role drift
    (SameFormulaDifferentWitnessRole,
    DifferentDecisionFunctional, etc.) are rejected with
    KindReasonIncompatible. This preserves the T.3 invariant
    that semantic_role_hash collapse only happens when role is
    preserved.
5.  Global-only kinds (WitnessLaw, NegativeWitnessLaw,
    LBandHonestyLaw, UsefulnessHonestyLaw, CorpusHashLaw,
    RegistryBindingLaw, ConstitutionLaw, DeferredGateLaw)
    MUST use the Global binding. Per-record kinds
    (DedupCanonical, AliasCollapse, CompositionJudgment,
    ParameterizationJudgment, SemanticRoleSeparation) MUST NOT.
    Mismatches are rejected with KindBindingIncompatible.
6.  Precedent ids are unique within the set (rejection kind:
    DuplicatePrecedentId).
