Panel-locked non-claims
----------------------------------------------------------------

T.12.consolidate does NOT:

  - Add new literature primitives. The expansion records still
    live in their T.12.x proposal modules; consolidate
    ratifies them as a deduplicated set.
  - Mutate SEED. The 54-record historical seed stays at 54.
  - Mutate corpus_hash_v1. The historical anchor stays
    canonical; corpus_hash_v1 becomes the historical seed-
    corpus anchor while corpus_hash_v2 becomes the first
    post-amendment corpus authority.
  - Mutate any prior T.11 / S1.3 / T.12.x hash. Every
    proposal's stored hash is verified by recomputation, not
    rewritten.
  - Promote individual proposals to Accepted. Every proposal
    stays at Open status; future per-proposal ratification
    commits change status.
  - Rebaseline R.12b D64 baseline. Pinned receipt restored
    from HEAD before commit; episodes byte-stable at
    13/89/1917.

corpus_hash_v2 is a META-hash over the ratified-expansion set;
it is NOT a full re-hash of a new SEED table. The migration
into a new SEED table is a separate future commit (gated on
per-proposal ProposalStatus::Accepted ratifications + FF.1
passport materialisation + FF.3 registry gate).
