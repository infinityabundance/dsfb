================================================================
T.12.l - Chemometrics amendment proposal summary
================================================================

Panel-locked thesis. T.12.l files the Chemometrics amendment
proposal. It admits only deterministic chemometric witnesses
whose chemometric model class, preprocessing law (baseline /
scatter / derivative / mean-centering), latent-space contract
(component-selection law, cross-validation policy), calibration
contract (reference quantity, calibration model, validation
law), residual definition, decision functional, and confuser /
nuisance profile are declared; resolves SEED collisions with
PCA T² / PCA SPE / PLS residual / Residual envelope exit;
classifies score-space outliers / latent-variable control
charts / spectral preprocessing artifacts as parameterizations;
records two domain transfers (PCA latent-space ancestor +
residual envelope-boundary ancestor); rejects black-box
spectroscopy classifiers and adaptive-AutoML chemometric
pipelines without deterministic seed / training-artifact /
cross-validation-policy anchors; and preserves the frozen T.10
corpus hash.

Main panel warning: "A chemometric witness is not a chemical
diagnosis, material identification, or regulatory-compliance
verdict unless the preprocessing law, model class, latent-space
contract, calibration contract, residual law, and decision
functional are declared."

Panel-locked non-claim (MUST appear verbatim):

> T.12.l admits deterministic chemometric residual / latent-
> space / calibration / concentration-structure witnesses. It
> does not admit chemical causation, material identification
> certainty, regulatory compliance, lab diagnosis, or process-
> control authority.
