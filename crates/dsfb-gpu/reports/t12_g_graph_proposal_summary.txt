================================================================
T.12.g - Graph / Topology Anomaly
        amendment proposal summary
================================================================

Panel-locked thesis. T.12.g files the Graph / Topology Anomaly
amendment proposal. It admits only deterministic topology
witnesses whose graph model, baseline, update law, metric
definition, normalization, node / edge identity law, and
decision functional are declared; resolves SEED collisions;
classifies metric variants as parameterizations; rejects
community / embedding / random-walk claims unless
deterministically reduced; and preserves the frozen T.10
corpus hash.

Main panel warning: "A graph metric is not a detector until
the baseline, update law, metric law, and decision law are
declared."
