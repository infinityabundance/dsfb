----------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Every rule (admission + confuser-suppression) MUST link at
    least one T.11b precedent. Empty linked_precedent_ids ⇒
    RuleWithoutPrecedentLink rejection.
2.  Every linked precedent id MUST resolve to an entry in the
    live T.11b PrecedentSet (rejection: LinkedPrecedentMissing).

    admission, either via the WitnessRequirement
    `forbids_confuser_only` flag OR via the
    `NoConfuserOnlyAdmission` evidence-requirement clause.
    Failure ⇒ EpisodeAdmissionAllowsConfuserOnly rejection.
    This is the load-bearing Semantic Non-Bypass invariant.
4.  Rule ids MUST be unique across admission + confuser rules
    (rejection: DuplicateRuleId).
