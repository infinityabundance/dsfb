Precedent linkage coverage
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Every grammar rule MUST link at least one T.11b precedent
  (panel-required invariant; pinned by
  `every_grammar_rule_links_precedent`).
- Every WitnessLaw precedent in T.11b is consumed by at least
  one grammar rule (panel-required;
  `every_witness_law_precedent_is_consumed`).
- Every NegativeWitnessLaw precedent in T.11b is consumed by at
  least one grammar rule (panel-required;
  `every_negative_witness_law_precedent_is_consumed`).

The SemanticNonBypass rules `BankAdmissionTokenIsTheOnlyAdmissionRoute`
and `GpuOutputIsEvidenceOnly` cite multiple precedents
(witness law + negative-witness law + constitution-coverage +
registry-binding) because they bind corpus-side jurisprudence
to runtime axioms.

Passport-precedent-grammar crosswalk (T.11a × T.11b × T.11c)
----------------------------------------------------------------
54 rows (one per SEED canonical record). Every row carries the
passport's `linked_precedent_ids` (from T.11b) PLUS the set of
grammar rules that consume any of those precedents. The Shewhart
crosswalk row (canonical_id 1) links to all 18 grammar rules
(9 admission + 9 confuser) because every global law touches
every passport AND the witness-law precedents propagate to
every confuser-suppression rule.

The crosswalk is the panel-required artifact for "every passport
links to a precedent that is consumed by a grammar rule" without
churning the passport struct or hash.
