Scope (panel-locked)
----------------------------------------------------------------
T.11g ships the court's **datasheet / model-card / safety-label
layer**. A detector is not fully admissible until the court
knows when it should not be trusted. Each canonical SEED record
gets one DetectorContraindicationReceiptV1 answering nine
panel-locked questions plus an adversarial-twin layer:

  1. works_best_when
  2. fails_when
  3. known_confusers
  4. required_sampling_law
  5. required_units
  6. minimum_support
  7. do_not_use_for
  8. closest_aliases
  9. closest_non_aliases

Plus the DetectorTwinRelation adversarial-twin axis with five
variants: SameFormulaDifferentRole, SameRoleDifferentFormula,
SameFamilyDifferentSamplingLaw, AliasLikeButSemanticallyDistinct,
ConfuserOfPrimary.

The receipt is built **deterministically from corpus SEED
metadata** (PrimitiveFamily / WitnessRole / InputRequirementSet /
ImplementationLevel) rather than hand-curated, so two builds
against the same SEED produce byte-identical hashes and the
verifier rules are satisfied by construction. Hand-curated
overrides land in T.11g.1+ if and when needed.

Scope-discipline lock (panel iteration 6, audit item 8)
----------------------------------------------------------------
T.11g absorbs UnitSemantics and SamplingLaw at the
receipt-level enum + verifier-rule layer ONLY. It does NOT
ship:
  - a unit-conversion engine (Celsius↔Fahrenheit, ms↔µs, ...);
  - a resampler / sampling-rate normaliser;
  - a numeric coercion engine;
  - ingestion-format changes;
  - any runtime behaviour that consumes the declarations.

Operational use of unit semantics (e.g. disabling a spectral
detector because sample_rate_hz is missing) is S1.3's
ActivationPlanner job, not T.11g's.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Reason-Code Coverage (running metric block, audit item 10)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The crosswalk table reports per-detector coverage of the nine
panel-locked questions. At T.11g, every receipt is built
deterministically from SEED metadata, so coverage is necessarily
100% by construction:

  every Primary witness        : known_confusers ≥ 1
  every Spectral detector      : required_sampling_law = Some
  every unit-sensitive detector: required_units = Some
  every distribution detector  : BaselineReferenceAvailable in works_best_when
  every time-series detector   : TimeOrderedInput / sampling-law declared
  every Active witness role    : do_not_use_for ≥ 1
  every L5/L6 detector         : required_sampling_law = Some

T.11h CoverageHoleReport will aggregate this block into the
cross-T.11 receipt-coverage table and surface domains, families,
or detector axes where the corpus has known gaps.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel-locked closing claim
----------------------------------------------------------------
A detector cannot be a serious court witness unless the court
knows its contraindications. T.11g makes that knowledge
verifier-enforced and replayable; the activation planner (S1.3)
will later consume these declarations to emit reason-coded
on/off decisions with real disqualifiers instead of vague
applicability logic.
