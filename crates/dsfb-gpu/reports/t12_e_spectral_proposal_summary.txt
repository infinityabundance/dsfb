================================================================
T.12.e - Signal Processing / Spectral / Wavelet
        amendment proposal summary
================================================================

Panel-locked thesis. T.12.e files the Signal Processing /
Spectral / Wavelet amendment proposal. It admits only
deterministic transform-based primitives whose sampling,
windowing, normalization, band, boundary, and template laws
are declared; resolves collisions with existing SEED records;
classifies transform variants as parameterizations; rejects
randomized or learned spectral claims unless deterministically
reduced; and preserves the frozen T.10 corpus hash.

Main panel warning: "In spectral detectors, the transform law
is the detector. No transform law, no canonical admission."

Transform-law contract discipline (panel-required)
----------------------------------------------------------------

Per the panel's main warning ("the transform law is the
detector"), every CanonicalAddition and
ExistingCanonicalAuthorityResolution record declares its
specific transform-law contract in the reason text. Each
declaration is pinned by a dedicated load-bearing negative:

  FFT band-energy (SEED 12) + FFT bandpower variant (5507) ->
    pinned by t12_e_rejects_fft_variant_without_window_and_
    normalization_law (MOST IMPORTANT for spectral)
  Spectral entropy (SEED 38) ->
    pinned by t12_e_rejects_spectral_entropy_without_bin_or_
    power_normalization_law
  Wavelet coefficient energy (SEED 39) + Wavelet packet
    energy (5502) -> pinned by t12_e_rejects_wavelet_detector_
    without_wavelet_family_and_boundary_law
  STFT ridge shift (5503) -> pinned by t12_e_rejects_stft_
    ridge_without_window_hop_and_ridge_selection_law
  Matched filter residual (5505) -> pinned by t12_e_rejects_
    matched_filter_without_template_provenance
  Hilbert amplitude anomaly (5506) -> pinned by t12_e_rejects_
    hilbert_amplitude_without_sampling_law
  Randomized spectral projection (5510 rejection) -> pinned by
    t12_e_rejects_randomized_spectral_projection_without_
    deterministic_reduction
