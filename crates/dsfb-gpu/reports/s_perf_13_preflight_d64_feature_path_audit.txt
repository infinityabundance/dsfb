 Why this preflight exists

The S-PERF.12a partial-success measurement (RTX 4080 SUPER,
CUDA 13.2, canonical 256x4096 K=1 D64, WARPS_PER_BLOCK=16,
7-iter median + 3-iter warm-up, 5-run steady-state) reported

The S-PERF.12a thesis projected post-S-PERF.13 bandwidth at
~26.8 GB/s assuming S-PERF.13 would attack a "host
compute_features" wall by moving feature construction to
device. The panel rule (verbatim) is:

  "Do not optimize a mislabeled bottleneck. First rename the
  bottleneck to what it actually measures. Then attack the
  real wall."

Therefore: before committing to a "device-side compute_features"
campaign, a read-only audit traced the D64 _timed dispatch
path from the public Rust entrypoint through the FFI into the
CUDA kernel implementation to confirm what the slot actually
measures and which CUDA kernels are actually launched.

 Math check (event-pack-to-pinned bandwidth)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  fixture: synthesize_scaled(DEFAULT_SEED, 256, 4096, 4)
  event count: 256 * 4096 * 4 = 4,194,304 events
  per-event packed size: 16 bytes (GpuTraceEventCompact)
  total packed bytes: 4,194,304 * 16 = 67,108,864 B = ~67 MB

  Modern DDR write bandwidth: ~50-100 GB/s
  Predicted lower bound (100 GB/s): 67 MB / 100 GB/s = ~670 us
  Predicted upper bound (50 GB/s):  67 MB / 50  GB/s = ~1340 us

  Measured features_us slot: ~6308 us (median, post-warmup)

  Ratio measured / predicted-upper: 6308 / 1340 ~= 4.7x

  Conclusion: the measured cost is ~4.7x what pure DDR
  bandwidth predicts. Most likely cause: the manual
  Rust iterator-chain loop

    for (dst, src) in slice.iter_mut().zip(events.iter())
        .take(events.len())
    {
        *dst = GpuTraceEventCompact::from_trace_event(src);
    }

  emits scalar stores that the compiler does not
  autovectorize. Each iteration is 1 u64 + 2 u32 stores
  (16 B), and the iterator zip chain may inhibit LLVM from
  recognising the bulk-store pattern. Inline AVX2
  intrinsics or a chunk-and-unroll refactor should recover
  most of the lost bandwidth.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 Approach selection
----------------------------------------------------------------

Approach A — SIMD-accelerated host pack (SELECTED, user-
            confirmed 2026-05-18; MEASURED OUTCOMES below).
  - Phase 1: chunk-and-unroll with #[inline(always)]
    helper; compiler autovectorization (LLVM AVX2 on
    x86_64 Haswell+). Projected: ~6.3 ms -> ~800-1500 us.
    **MEASURED**: 6308 us -> 7019 us (NEGATIVE result; the
    iterator-chain refactor inhibited autovectorization
    further than the scalar baseline). Reason: the
    structure-conversion shape (TraceEvent 48 B with
    scattered field reads at {0, 8, 32, 38} -> 16 B
    contiguous write) resists LLVM auto-SIMD because of
    the conditional error-bit branch + cross-struct
    field gathering. Phase 1 ESCALATED to Phase 2.
  - Phase 2 (escalation if Phase 1 misses the gate):
    inline core::arch::x86_64 AVX2 intrinsics (stable
    since 2017, portable across every x86_64 host since
    Haswell). Projected: ~500 us. Gated behind
    #[cfg(target_feature = "avx2")] with a scalar
    fallback.
    **MEASURED**: 6308 us -> ~5700 us (NINE-PERCENT
    improvement, byte-identical contract held, episodes
    1917 preserved). Implementation: pair-wise 256-bit
    stores via _mm256_stream_si256 (non-temporal) when
    dst is 32 B aligned (production cudaMallocHost
    path), _mm256_storeu_si256 fallback for misaligned
    Vec<T> (unit tests). The 5700 us measurement is
    ~1.1x the theoretical 5200 us DDR4 memory-bandwidth
    floor for 267 MB / dispatch (4.2M events x (48 B
    read + 16 B write)). The staging slot is now at the
    memory-bandwidth equilibrium; further SIMD tuning
    cannot extract additional savings.

Approach B — Caller-materialised compact projection.
  REJECTED. Just moves the cost outside the timer rather
  than reducing real per-catalog work. Verifier negative
  N7 forbids it as the S-PERF.13 lever.

Approach C — Device-side event projection kernel.
  REJECTED. Triples PCIe traffic (200 MB vs 67 MB at
  4.2M events); at PCIe Gen4 x16 (~16 GB/s sustained)
  that is ~12.5 ms — likely a net regression.

    Bandwidth is in a thermal-variance band; the host wall

    structural floor is memory-bandwidth-bound staging
    + device-stage time dominance (65% of wall).

 Honest structural diagnosis (panel-locked, 2026-05-18)
----------------------------------------------------------------

The S-PERF.13 staging-acceleration lever does NOT clear the
S-PERF.12 promotion gate by itself. The math:

  Pre-S-PERF.13 wall:  ~23,388 us  -> 18.72 GB/s
  Phase 2 staging:     6308 -> 5700 (~9% win)
  Phase 2 wall:        ~22,780 us  -> ~19.2 GB/s (projected;
                                       thermal-noise-bound)
  Gate target:         <= 21,900 us -> >= 20 GB/s
  Missing:             ~880 us still to shave

Where the remaining ~880 us must come from:

  Device wall (post-S-PERF.12a stage breakdown, ~15,278 us):
    h2d:                             2507 us  (16.4%)
    detector_motif_kernel_wide_d64:  2702 us  (17.7%)
    compact_densor consensus:        2402 us  (15.7%)
    compact_densor sign:             1530 us  (10.0%)
    compact_densor detector:         1401 us  (9.2%)
    drift_slew_sign_kernel:          1284 us  (8.4%)
    compact_densor residual:         1254 us  (8.2%)
    candidate_collapse_kernel_wide:  1120 us  (7.3%)
    other (residual / consensus / axis5 / d2h): ~1078 us

  Total compact_densor digest stages: 6587 us (43.1% of
  device wall). Fusing the 4 leaf kernels into a single
  multi-stream kernel could save 1500-2500 us.

  Other fusion candidates: residual+sign (saves 300-500 us),
  consensus+axis5_grid_sum+candidate_collapse (saves
  500-1000 us). H2D pipelining is K=1-blocked at this
  fixture; needs batched dispatch to benefit.
