Panel-required load-bearing negatives (6)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R.1  ActivationForUnratifiedProposal
     Reject any decision whose canonical id is outside SEED
     AND outside the ratified expansion index AND whose status
     is NOT UnratifiedProposal. Surfaces the "id in nowhere"
     bucket.
     Test: ff2_rejects_activation_for_unratified_proposal

R.2  ActivationForMissingFf1Passport
     Reject any decision whose canonical id is in the ratified
     expansion index AND NOT in the FF.1 passport index AND
     whose status is NOT MissingPassport. Surfaces the "ratified
     but no passport" structural defect.
     Test: ff2_rejects_activation_for_missing_ff1_passport

R.3  PassportIndexHashMismatch
     Reject when the gate's pinned ff1_passport_index_hash_v1
     does not equal the live FF.1 passport index hash. Surfaces
     stale-anchor defects (the FF.1 passport surface rotated
     under FF.2's feet).
     Test: ff2_rejects_activation_when_passport_index_hash_mismatch

R.4  UnratifiedProposalWithoutReasonCode
     Reject any decision whose status is UnratifiedProposal or
     MissingPassport AND whose disabled_reason_wire_name is
     empty. Surfaces the "non-ratified status without explicit
     reason code" defect.
     Test: ff2_rejects_unratified_proposal_without_reason_code

R.5  SilentFallbackToDisabledByWeakLBand
     Reject any decision whose status is UnratifiedProposal or
     MissingPassport AND whose disabled_reason_wire_name is
     NOT "DisabledUnratifiedProposal" (specifically rejecting
     "DisabledByWeakLBand" — the pre-FF.2 silent fallback the
     panel warning forbids). The panel verdict is explicit:
     unratified proposals must not collapse into the generic
     weak-L-band bucket.
     Test: ff2_rejects_silent_fallback_to_disabled_by_weak_lband

R.6  ActivationReasonWithoutCorpusHashV2Binding
     Reject when the gate's corpus_hash_v2 field is the all-
     zero sentinel OR does not equal the live consolidation
     report's corpus_hash_v2. The gate MUST anchor to the
     ratified-corpus authority.
     Test: ff2_rejects_activation_reason_without_corpus_hash_v2_binding

Structural defect rules (4)
----------------------------------------------------------------

R.7  DuplicateGateDecisionForSameCanonicalId
     Reject two decisions for the same canonical id.
     Test: ff2_rejects_duplicate_gate_decision_for_same_canonical_id

R.8  GateDecisionsNotSortedAscending
     Reject when decisions are not sorted strictly ascending
     by canonical id (the gate hash material requires
     canonical ordering for byte stability).
     Test: ff2_rejects_gate_decisions_not_sorted_ascending

R.9  CorpusHashV1Mismatch
     Reject when the gate's corpus_hash_v1 does not equal
     compute_corpus_hash_v1(). Surfaces a defect where FF.2
     accidentally read a stale corpus snapshot.
     Test: ff2_rejects_corpus_hash_v1_mismatch

R.10 ConsolidationReportHashV1Mismatch
     Reject when the gate's consolidation_report_hash_v1 does
     not equal the live consolidation report hash. Surfaces
     a defect where FF.2 read a stale T.12.consolidate hash.
     Test: ff2_rejects_consolidation_report_hash_v1_mismatch

R.10b SEED-invariance (unconditional)
     Reject when SEED.len() != 54.
     Implicitly covered by every default-build invariant test
     (the FF.2 builder rejects construction with the wrong
     SEED length).
