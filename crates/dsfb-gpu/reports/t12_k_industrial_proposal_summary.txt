T.12.k - Industrial / Fault Detection and Diagnostics /
        Condition Monitoring amendment proposal summary
================================================================

Panel-locked thesis. T.12.k files the Industrial / FDD /
Condition Monitoring amendment proposal. It admits only
deterministic condition-monitoring / FDD witnesses whose plant
or sensor model, physical quantity, unit law, sampling law,
operating regime, baseline / nominal envelope, residual
definition, fault-signature decision law, and confuser /
nuisance-process profile are declared; resolves SEED
collisions; classifies variants as parameterizations or domain
transfers; rejects proprietary PdM black-box scores and learned
fault classifiers without training-artifact anchors; and
preserves the frozen T.10 corpus hash.

Main panel warning: "An industrial fault witness is not a
diagnosis of machine cause unless the plant model, residual
law, sensor law, operating regime, and confuser profile are
declared."

Panel-locked non-claim (MUST appear verbatim):

> witnesses, not root-cause certainty and not maintenance
> recommendations.
