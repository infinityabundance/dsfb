=== STEP 0 LESSON RECORDED (panel-discipline) ===

The A5 → A6 transition is the canonical worked example for the rule:
"source-level caching of per-thread reads through indexed local arrays
is not local-memory caching — it is per-thread GLOBAL-memory staging
that the compiler scalarizes away under __forceinline__, defeating the
expected L1 LOAD reduction." A5 v1 / v2 / v2.1 hit the same bit-exact
2,339,556,672 floor across three independent attempts because the
underlying read pattern was structurally identical to legacy. A6.1
eliminated the structural amplification, not the source-level pattern.
This lesson is recorded as feedback memory for future panel-locked
optimization sub-legs.
