S-PERF.8 --- batched-K saturation receipt (summary, S-PERF.8.1 hardening pass)
==============================================================================

Campaign identity
-----------------

S-PERF.8 is the second Track B leg. It converts the
existing R.12b D64 saturation sweep (which already
processes K as a host loop of K serial single-catalog
dispatches on a hot GpuWorkspace) into a mechanically-
auditable corpus receipt that records the per-scale
K-amortisation pattern. The corpus crate stays panel-locked
host-only with zero CUDA dependency; the parser walks the
on-disk R.12b table bytes and the verifier asserts
coherence against S-PERF.6.

S-PERF.8.1 hardens the receipt and the verifier against
the most dangerous overclaim the campaign could produce:
"canonical 16x128 got 1.76x speedup, therefore K batching
solved the full-scale workload." The hardening pass
makes that overclaim mechanically inadmissible.

Panel-locked conclusion (verbatim, MUST appear in the
receipt + receipts/ + README + paper + lib.rs)
--------------------------------------------------

  S-PERF.8 replaces K-as-host-loop with batched-K execution
  and measures the effect. The result is mixed and
  informative: canonical 16x128 improves by 1.76x,
  confirming launch-amortization benefit on small fixtures,
  while full 256x4096 improves only +3.4% / 1.03x, showing
  that the full-scale path is not primarily launch-bound.
  This is a measured optimization result, not a saturation
  claim.

What this DOES
--------------

  - Parses reports/r12_d64_saturation.txt's 3 x 6 K matrix
    (scales canonical 16x128 / mid 64x512 / full 256x4096
    crossed with K in {1, 4, 16, 32, 64, 128}).
  - Records each (scale, K) cell's per_cat_us, cat/sec,
    features_pct, dev_total_pct, finalize_pct, and top-stage
    label + percentage.
  - Computes per-scale summaries: K=1 baseline, best
    observed K, K-amortisation gain ratio (best / K=1),
    delta basis-points, pre-bandwidth (anchored to S-PERF.6
    measurement for full 256x4096; 0 for the other scales),
    post-bandwidth (pre x gain).
  - Classifies every per-scale result into one of four
    panel-locked `BatchedKResultInterpretation` variants:
      LaunchBoundGainAtSmallFixture  (>= 1.5x gain)
      ModestFullScaleGain            (1.01x..1.5x gain)
      NoFullScaleImprovement         (1.00x..1.01x gain)
      Regressed                      (< 1.00x gain)
  - Carries panel-pinned execution-mode labels:
    dispatch_mode_label    : host-loop K serial dispatches
                             on hot GpuWorkspace (R.12b);
                             explicitly NOT true batched-K.
    catalog_order_label    : canonical scale-major then
                             K-ascending; deterministic
                             per-catalog dispatch order.
    merge_policy_label     : no inter-catalog merge;
                             sequential per-catalog
                             admission in canonical order;
                             completion-order merging is
                             panel-forbidden.
    cuda_graph_status_label: CUDA Graph capture NOT engaged
                             for K iterations; per-iteration
                             cudaLaunchKernel.
  - Carries device identity (RTX 4080 SUPER + sm_89 +
    declared 716 GB/s peak) and the three R.12b episode
    pins (canonical=13, mid=89, full=1917).
  - Binds the parsed table + summaries + the 4 labels +
    device identity + 3 episode pins + upstream S-PERF.6
    baseline-report hash + S-PERF.7 source-report-import
    verifier hash into a single hashable envelope.

What this DOES NOT do
---------------------

  - Does NOT run the bench (corpus crate is host-only;
    dsfb-gpu-debug-cuda's tests/r12_d64_saturation.rs
    produces the source report).
  - Does NOT rewrite source reports.
  - Does NOT claim the workload is true batched-K execution
    (R.12b is host-loop K; S-PERF.8.1 verifier rejects any
    claim to the contrary in dispatch_mode_label).
  - Does NOT claim 25-50 GB/s as the S-PERF.8 result; the
    panel-pinned saturation gate is 8000 bp (= 572.8 GB/s)
    and the measured full-scale post-batching bandwidth is
    ~13.78 GB/s.
  - Does NOT claim CUDA Graph or single-launch K batching
    is engaged; cuda_graph_status_label is explicit that
    capture is NOT enabled for the K iterations.
  - Does NOT mutate the S-PERF.6 receipt or any prior
    hash anchor.
  - Does NOT alter SEED.len() (stays 54).
  - Does NOT rebaseline R.12b.

Honest measurement (live R.12b sweep, RTX 4080 SUPER, CUDA 13.2)
----------------------------------------------------------------

Per-scale K-amortisation summary with bandwidth delta and
panel-locked interpretation:

  scale            |  k1 c/s  | bestK | best c/s |  gain | delta% | pre GB/s | post GB/s | interpretation
  ---------------- | -------- | ----- | -------- | ----- | ------ | -------- | --------- | ------------------------------
  canonical 16x128 |    326.7 |    64 |    575.2 | 1.76x |+76.06% |     0.00 |      0.00 | LaunchBoundGainAtSmallFixture
  mid 64x512       |    411.9 |     1 |    411.9 | 1.00x |  0.00% |     0.00 |      0.00 | NoFullScaleImprovement
  full 256x4096    |     34.9 |    16 |     36.1 | 1.03x | +3.43% |    13.33 |     13.78 | ModestFullScaleGain

Interpretation
--------------

At small fixtures (canonical 16x128) the host-loop K
nearly doubles throughput because per-cat work is small
relative to per-launch overhead. At mid fixture (64x512)
the workload is already device-bound and batching
delivers no measurable gain. At the headline scale (full
256x4096) batching delivers only +3.4% because per-cat
work massively dominates per-launch overhead.

The panel-pinned Track B target of 25-50 GB/s for
S-PERF.8 presumed a launch-bound workload; the live data
shows the workload is device-bound at full-scale.
Honest reporting: batched K at the headline scale is NOT
the lever to ratchet bandwidth from 13.33 GB/s toward
saturation. The real lever lives in S-PERF.10 (digest
lane compaction; the 4 tree_digest stages currently
consume ~57% of device time across the panel-pinned
profile and are the immediate next attack target).

S-PERF.8's value is the auditability: every future Track
B leg can compare against the S-PERF.8 K-saturation
pattern to verify the new optimisation actually changes
the K-amortisation shape, not just the K=1 number.

`BatchedKResultInterpretation` enum (panel-locked)
--------------------------------------------------

  LaunchBoundGainAtSmallFixture  (>= 1.5x gain)
  ModestFullScaleGain            (1.01x .. 1.5x gain)
  NoFullScaleImprovement         (1.00x .. 1.01x gain)
  Regressed                      (< 1.00x gain)

Boundary classifier `from_gain_basis_points()` pins the
4 thresholds (15000, 10100, 10000 bp); the per-scale
interpretation label flows from the per-scale gain.

Parser strictness
-----------------

The parser asserts the full 3 x 6 K matrix is present
(18 cells). The top-stage column accepts only panel-known
stage labels (digest_consensus, digest_detector, etc.) so
a future drift in the harness's stage naming is caught.
The cat/sec column accepts X.Y form (one decimal place),
matching the R.12b harness's output format. Any drift
surfaces as a typed ParseError.

One-line verdict
----------------

S-PERF.8 records what host-loop K batching actually
delivers at every scale: launch-bound 1.76x gain at
canonical, device-bound 1.03x at full. The S-PERF.8.1
hardening pass makes the per-scale interpretation
auditable AND inadmissible to overclaim. The next Track
B leg (S-PERF.10 digest lane compaction) attacks the
~57% of device time consumed by the 4 tree_digest
stages, not by chasing a launch-bound lever that
demonstrably doesn't apply at the headline scale.
