----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel-locked non-claims
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - S1.2 does NOT activate detectors. active_detectors = 0.
  - S1.2 does NOT execute GPU kernels. admitted_episodes = 0.
  - S1.2 does NOT claim that every spec is empirically useful.
    Per-task utility is recorded by the T.8 usefulness ledger.
  - S1.2 does NOT generate 2,000 detectors. The 162-spec count
    is the disciplined first pass; the wider grid lands as
    S1.2.1+ after planner / dispatcher infrastructure matures.
  - S1.2 declares ImplementationKind::ScalarCpu on every spec
    regardless of corpus L-band. The five L6 corpus records
    (canonical IDs 14, 15, 41, 42, 43 — the dsfb-gpu-debug-core
    bank surface) DO have GPU implementations, but the S1.2
    DetectorSpec declaration does NOT inherit the GPU surface
    because the registry generator has no GPU dispatch layer
    yet.
