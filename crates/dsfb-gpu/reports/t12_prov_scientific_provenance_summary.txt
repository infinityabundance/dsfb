----------------------------------------------------------------
Court-delta posture
----------------------------------------------------------------

T.12.PROV is derivation-only: it walks the live T.12.a..T.12.p
proposal set through `consolidate::load_all_t12_proposals` and
emits three artifacts without mutating any prior hash.

  - ScientistCreditIndex
    One ScientistCredit row per CanonicalAddition (98 total),
    sorted ascending by canonical id. Each row carries:
    detector_name, source_class_wire_name, origin_proposal_id,
    source_ref_keys list, contribution text (from the dedup
    record's reason), motivation text (from the matching
    ProposedPrimitive), and the panel-locked DSFB credit
    note.

  - SourceBibliographyIndex
    One bibliography entry per unique (citation_key,
    source_class) pair (133 total), sorted ascending by
    (source_class_wire_name, citation_key). Records the
    citation key, title, year, venue, source class, and
    origin proposal id.

  - ProvenanceCreditReport (top-level wrapper)
    META-hashes both indexes plus corpus_hash_v1, SEED.len(),
    proposal_count, rejection_record_count,
    parameterization_record_count, and the panel-locked
    credit note. One hash pins the entire T.12.PROV
    provenance pass.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Eight panel-required load-bearing negatives
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. t12_prov_rejects_canonical_addition_without_scientist_credit
       — every CanonicalAddition dedup record MUST have a
         matching ProposedPrimitive in the same batch.
  2. t12_prov_rejects_canonical_addition_without_source_ref
       — every CanonicalAddition's batch MUST carry at least
         one ProposedSourceRef.
  3. t12_prov_rejects_scientist_credit_without_contribution_note
       — every CanonicalAddition's reason text MUST be
         non-empty.
  4. t12_prov_rejects_source_ref_key_not_in_proposal_sources
       — every source_ref_key on a credit row MUST appear in
         the originating proposal's proposed_source_refs list.
  5. t12_prov_rejects_dsfb_invention_claim_for_prior_detector
       — case-insensitive scanner forbids phrases like
         "dsfb invented", "dsfb-gpu-atlas invented",
         "we invented", "atlas invented", "originally /
         first introduced/proposed by dsfb" in any
         CanonicalAddition motivation or contribution text.
  6. t12_prov_rejects_engineering_practice_record_without_provenance_note
       — every ProposedSourceRef with year=0 (engineering
         practice) MUST carry a non-empty venue / provenance
         note.
  7. t12_prov_rejects_rejected_record_without_method_family_credit
       — every RejectedNotDeterministic dedup record MUST
         carry a non-empty reason (the reason credits the
         method family being refused, even though not
         admitted).
  8. t12_prov_rejects_parameterization_without_parent_lineage_note
       — every ParameterizationOf dedup record MUST carry a
         non-empty reason (the reason names the parent
         canonical the parameterization collapses into).
