**Surprising calibrations vs the PHASE0_SURFACE.md plan-stated order.**
- **M.3.J (Slice) was sized as a major pass** ("Heavy in `phase0_compiler.phos` because of `Slice[u8, 65536]`") but only **1 fn body** uses Slice indexing. The other 15 Slice usages are in *type signatures* — no body lowering needed for them; the producer just needs to accept the syntax at the parser layer.
- **M.3.K (Result/Option/struct/enum decls) was sized similarly** but only **1 fn body** uses Result/Option constructors. Same story: most usage is in return-type signatures.
- **M.3.G-full and M.3.H-full are co-equal top blockers (71% each).** PHASE0_SURFACE.md ordered M.3.H-full before M.3.G-full; the data says they're equal-priority. Whichever lands first unblocks half the corpus, and the second closes the rest.
- **`if-expr` was not in the plan** — and indeed phase0_compiler.phos uses `match` exclusively, so it stays unlisted.
